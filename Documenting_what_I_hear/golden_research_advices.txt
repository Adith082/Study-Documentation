how to find research gaps quickly and efficiently?

->go to google scholar

-> find recent research gaps (not older)

-> in the search bar, go for recent (2-3 years old papers search by relevancy 
(dont just always look at review papers because that would call out even older papers))

-> look for top conference , journal papers(with high citations) (like CVPR, nature etc.)

-> go straight to the study limitations (or might be in the discussion) section where future research, work limitations, weaknesses etc. will be mentioned.

-> out of all the gaps, try to find the one that can be a "low hanging fruit" as well as the most important gaps you think that need to be addressed

-> there can be several types of gaps such as 
       --> methological gap or evidence gap
       --> population gap 

-> you may collect bunch of these gaps, but it is important to find important, feasible ones
and the ones that you could actually address in your convenient time. so for that, you could
communicate with your mentors about these gaps and have clear idea to execute your work further.

-> take a more systematic approach yourself. do systematic review, write systematic review 
papers(set agenda for future works) and publish that paper in top journal!
